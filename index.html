    How this league is won

    1. Steals and blocks are worth 3. One block equals three points scored. Lean toward players tagged STOCKS when ranks are close.
    2. Missed shots cost nothing. There's no FG% or FT% penalty, so minutes and volume beat efficiency. Bad free-throw shooters are fine here.
    3. Games played decides weeks. Lineups are daily and you get 4 adds a week, so durable players beat fragile ones at the same rank.
    4. Only 8 teams, so the waiver wire is deep. Take the best player, not the position you need. Upside beats safety in later rounds, and your 4 weekly adds are a real weapon for streaming.
    5. Two C slots. Get two real centers by about round 7; bigs stack rebounds and blocks.
    6. IL slots are free roster space. In rounds 12–13, take an injured player who returns before the playoffs (tagged STASH) and park him on IL once he's listed out.
    7. Mark every pick as it happens. Tap Gone for others' picks and Mine for yours so the board and your next pick stay accurate.
